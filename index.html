Consumer Protection Punjab, Pakistan Effective: 2005 Status: Active Jurisdiction: Provincial
Section 3: Rights of Consumers
Every consumer shall have the right to be protected against goods and services which are hazardous to life, property or safety. Any consumer aggrieved by a defect may lodge a complaint before the Consumer Court having territorial jurisdiction. The Court shall adjudicate disputes expeditiously within 6 months.

Section 3: Rights of Consumers & Remedies
(1) Every consumer shall have the right to be protected against goods and services which are hazardous to life, property or safety.
(2) Where a defect in products causes loss to a consumer, the manufacturer and retailer shall be jointly and severally liable.
(3) The Consumer Court may award punitive damages not exceeding fifty thousand rupees in addition to actual loss.
