Police Station Challenge

Police Refusal to Register an FIR

The local police station officer refuses to register your robbery or theft report.

Statutory Remedy: Under Section 154 Cr.P.C., the police are legally bound to register cognizable complaints. If refused, file a Section 22-A/22-B application before the Ex-Officio Justice of Peace (Sessions Court) or lodge a direct private complaint under Section 200 Cr.P.C.
Cr.P.C § 154 Cr.P.C § 22-A
Digital Safety

Online Blackmail & Private Photo Leaks

Someone is threatening to share private photographs or blackmailing you on WhatsApp.

Statutory Remedy: Under Section 21 of PECA 2016, transmitting explicit material without consent carries up to 5 years imprisonment and 5 million PKR fine. Immediate complaint must be submitted to the FIA Cyber Crime Wing (NR3C) for forensic evidence preservation under Section 31.
PECA § 21 PECA § 24
Consumer Rights

Shopkeeper Refuses to Return Defective Appliance

You bought an electronic appliance that stopped working within warranty, but the seller refuses replacement.

Statutory Remedy: Serve a mandatory 15-day written legal notice under Section 13 of the Punjab Consumer Protection Act 2005. If unresolved, lodge a claim in the District Consumer Court for full refund plus damages. No court fee is required.
Consumer Act § 13 Consumer Act § 3
Constitutional Safeguard

Arbitrary Police Detention & Bail Rights

A family member has been detained by police for over 24 hours without being presented before a Magistrate.

Statutory Remedy: Article 10 of the Constitution mandates that no citizen shall be detained beyond 24 hours without the authority of a Magistrate. For bail in non-bailable offences, apply under Section 497 Cr.P.C. Statutory delay of 1 year warrants automatic bail.
Constitution Art 10 Cr.P.C § 497

Full Statutory Text
Section 3. Rights of Consumers. (1) Every consumer shall have the right to be protected against goods and services which are hazardous to life, property or safety. Any consumer aggrieved by a defect may lodge a complaint before the Consumer Court having territorial jurisdiction.

Section 4. Liability for defective products. (1) The manufacturer of a product shall be liable to a consumer for damages caused by a characteristic of the product that renders the product defective.

Section 11. Duty of disclosure. (1) Where the nature of the product is such that the disclosure of its components or characteristics is necessary, the manufacturer shall display such information on the package.
