Full Statutory Text
Section 3. Rights of Consumers. (1) Every consumer shall have the right to be protected against goods and services which are hazardous to life, property or safety. Any consumer aggrieved by a defect may lodge a complaint before the Consumer Court having territorial jurisdiction.

Section 4. Liability for defective products. (1) The manufacturer of a product shall be liable to a consumer for damages caused by a characteristic of the product that renders the product defective.

Section 11. Duty of disclosure. (1) Where the nature of the product is such that the disclosure of its components or characteristics is necessary, the manufacturer shall display such information on the package.
